The Challenge

Why Touchless Matters in Hospitals

Hospital-acquired infections remain one of the leading causes of preventable patient death. Traditional touch interfaces in sterile environments are a silent threat.

🦠

Cross-Contamination Risk

Surgeons and nurses touching screens during procedures can transfer pathogens to patients, equipment, and surfaces.

🧤

Sterile Gown Compromise

Gloved hands interacting with standard interfaces breach sterile protocols, requiring re-gloving and costly delays.

⏱️

Workflow Interruptions

Staff must step away from critical tasks to operate traditional systems, impacting patient safety and care efficiency.

Technology

How Leap Motion Enables This

The Leap Motion Controller uses infrared cameras and optical sensors to track hands and fingers in 3D space with sub-millimeter accuracy — no contact required.

1

Sense

Leap Motion infrared sensors detect hand position, orientation, and individual finger joints.

→
2

Interpret

Python middleware maps raw skeletal data to gestures: swipe, point, grab, pinch.

→
3

Execute

Flutter frontend receives commands via WebSocket and updates the UI in real time.

→
4

Feedback

Audio beep + visual highlight confirm the action without requiring touch confirmation.

Supported Gestures

Gesture Vocabulary

👋

Swipe

Navigate between pages, dismiss alerts, scroll through records.

☝️

Point / Select

Hover finger over element for 1 second to trigger selection.

🤏

Pinch

Zoom into patient records, medical images, or vital charts.

✊

Grab / Hold

Confirm critical actions (drug administration, alarm acknowledgement).
